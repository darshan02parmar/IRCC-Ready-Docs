# Home

**Live:** [https://docs.getnorthpath.com/](https://docs.getnorthpath.com/)

The homepage is the **IRCC Ready Docs hub**. It introduces the free toolkit, links every major section, and answers common questions. No signup is required to open a tool.

---

## What you do here

1. Scan the hero and pick **Browse all 15 tools** or **Get a free template**
2. Jump to Tools, Templates, Guides, IMM Forms, Checklists, or Countries
3. Open a live tool that runs in your browser
4. Read the FAQ (files stay on device, not affiliated with IRCC, 4 MB limit)
5. Optionally explore the Get North Path family or book a free consult
